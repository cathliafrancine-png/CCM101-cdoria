# Mission Reflection

## 1. Which cloud platform impressed you the most?

The cloud platform that impressed me the most is AWS because of its wide range of cloud services and global infrastructure. I found it useful that AWS provides different services for computing, storage, databases, networking, and other business needs. Its flexibility makes it suitable for different types of organizations and applications.

## 2. What similarities exist among AWS, Azure, and GCP?

AWS, Azure, and GCP all provide cloud services that allow organizations to use computing resources over the internet. They all offer equivalent services for virtual machines, object storage, databases, identity management, and Kubernetes. All three platforms also provide scalable infrastructure that can support different workloads.

## 3. How do business requirements influence cloud platform selection?

Business requirements are important when selecting a cloud platform because different organizations have different priorities. Factors such as budget, existing technologies, performance, scalability, AI requirements, and global availability can affect the decision. The best provider is the one that can meet the organization's specific needs.

## 4. What did you learn from acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant?

I learned that choosing a cloud provider is not simply about selecting the most popular platform. A Cloud Solutions Consultant needs to understand the client's requirements and compare the services and strengths of different providers. I also learned that technical decisions should be connected to the business goals of the client.

## 5. How has your GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio improved after completing this mission?

My GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio improved because I added a complete Multi-Cloud Explorer laboratory with organized Markdown documentation. I documented my research about AWS, Azure, and GCP, compared their services, created recommendations, and included evidence from my Linux investigation. This made my portfolio more organized and showed my understanding of cloud computing concepts.
